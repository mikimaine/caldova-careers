# Story 1.3: Show how many open roles each department has

<!-- foundry-story: story_01a21067-975c-76be-95e2-266fa60c4b14 -->

Epic: none

## Story

As a job seeker, I want each department in the filter to show how many open roles it has, so that I know where the openings are before I choose.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Given the listing, when the department filter shows, then each department option reads its name followed by its open role count in parentheses, such as Finance (2). <!-- ac_01a21067-975c-7907-bff5-5f554528d28d -->
- Given the listing, when the filter shows All departments, then that option reads the total count of open roles. <!-- ac_01a21067-975c-7740-89e0-b0e85652d339 -->
